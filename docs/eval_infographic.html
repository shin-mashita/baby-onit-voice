2 · Prompt (verbatim, versioned in repo)

SYSTEM_PROMPT (baby_onit.py:499) + VOICE_PROMPT (baby_onit.py:522) — excerpt
You are baby-onit, a helpful AI agent that completes tasks by calling tools. ... You are speaking out loud. Everything you write is sent to a speech synthesizer and heard by a person: no markdown, no URLs read character by character. Short sentences — the synthesizer speaks one at a time. Speaking is a tool call: `speak`. Call it as your FIRST action on any task that will take more than a moment. Your final answer is spoken too. A voice activity detector is listening the whole time you talk. If the person starts speaking, your speech is cut off mid-sentence — that is normal; do not apologise or try to resume where you left off.

3 · Context Engineering

GOES IN
  • System prompt + VOICE_PROMPT (voice mode)
  • Turn text from VAD / TurnDetector
  • Tool results (speak, files, web, home)
  • Session history (opt-in, --resume)
MANAGED
  • One system message, assembled once, never mutated mid-loop
  • Pre-emptive compaction at 0.85× max_context_tokens
  • _fit_messages() hard trim, last resort
  • Replayed history decays; live turn never truncated
  • Background tasks: isolated history, no cross-talk

Network vs. Compute

computeASR, TTS — tunnel round trip is ~6-10 ms; stage latency is 200-750 ms. GPU decode/synthesis dominates, not the network.

networkLLM TTFT — Portkey's own time-to-first-byte varied 0.3-2.3 s on a request with no model compute. Explains the 3.1 s p95 (cold-start) directly.

localBarge-in — VAD, flush, and cancel are all on-Pi. No round trip on this path; the figure is a configured threshold, not network latency.
